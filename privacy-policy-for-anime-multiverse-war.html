1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Anime: Multiverse War (the “Game”) handles information when you play, view advertisements, access online configuration, or use More Games. The Game is developed and published by Wilson Dev. This policy also describes the Wilson Dev informational website. Privacy contact: fajarimam67@gmail.com. Developer / publisher: Wilson Dev.

2. Information processed

  • Game progress and preferences: progress, unlocked content, currency, control layouts, audio settings, and similar preferences are stored on your device for gameplay.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect and share IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising or other device identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic/performance data. The exact processing depends on the SDK version, device settings, and consent choices.
  • Online requests: fetching remote settings and More Games icons exposes network information such as your IP address and request metadata to the configuration or image host. These services may record technical access logs.
  • Support: if you contact us, we process the contact details, message, and diagnostic information you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. How information is used and shared

Information is used to operate the Game, save preferences, retrieve settings and promotional content, deliver and measure ads, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. AdMob processes information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Information is shared with the providers involved in those functions and may be disclosed when required by applicable law. More Games cards labelled “ADS” promote other apps; tapping one opens an external store or website. Loading a remote icon also sends a request to its host before you tap a card.

4. Service providers and external links

The Game uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy messaging. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps. External stores, advertised apps, and linked websites apply their own privacy policies. This website has no added analytics, ad scripts, forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages, its hosting provider, may process visitors’ IP addresses and technical logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Privacy choices

Where applicable, an advertising consent message allows you to make choices before ads are requested. If a privacy-options entry point is provided in the Game, use it to review your choices. You can also manage, reset, or delete the advertising ID using available Android privacy settings. Disabling personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising or technical data processing. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing and to withdraw consent. Contact us to submit a request; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

6. Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain until the app data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled; device backup settings may retain a copy. Clearing local data can permanently remove progress and does not erase data held by advertising or hosting providers. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request, handle follow-up, and meet applicable legal obligations. Technical logs and advertising data are retained under the relevant provider’s policies. Contact the privacy address below for deletion requests relating to data under our control; we may need limited verification. For provider-held data, use the provider’s privacy controls or request channels.

7. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No system is completely secure. Providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable protections. We cannot directly access or delete every identifier or record held by an independent provider.

8. Children’s privacy

Advertising and data handling must match the audience declared for the Game on Google Play. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information should contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion of information under our control where required. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about a child.

9. Policy updates

We may revise this policy when the Game or its data practices change. The updated policy and revision date will be published here. Where required, additional notice or consent will be provided.
