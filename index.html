About Wilson Dev

Small details.
Better moments of play.

Wilson Dev is an independent game developer focused on creating enjoyable experiences for Android players. We believe a good mobile game starts with a clear idea, responsive controls, and gameplay that feels rewarding to learn.

Our work brings together game design, visual presentation, sound, and practical mobile interfaces. Whether you are learning a character’s abilities, finding a better way through a stage, or preparing for a difficult boss encounter, we want each part of the experience to feel connected.

Development is an ongoing process. We value player feedback because it helps us understand what feels enjoyable, what needs more clarity, and where the experience can improve. Our goal is to keep refining the details that matter during real play.

01 / GAMEPLAY

Action with intention

We aim to create encounters where movement, timing, and learning your abilities make a meaningful difference. Each challenge should give you a reason to stay involved.

02 / MOBILE DESIGN

Comfort in your hands

Mobile screens deserve thoughtful interfaces. We pay attention to readable information, accessible controls, and layouts that help players focus on the game.

03 / DEVELOPMENT

Room to keep improving

We use feedback and testing to guide improvements, from clearer menus and better animation to more balanced encounters and a smoother overall experience.

Helpful Information

A few things you may want to know.

What does Wilson Dev create?

Wilson Dev develops Android games with a focus on enjoyable gameplay, action, and mobile-friendly controls. This website introduces our projects and provides access to support and game-specific privacy information.

How can I report a problem with a game?

Email fajarimam67@gmail.com with the game name, your device model, Android version, and a description of the problem. If possible, include the steps that caused it and a screenshot. Please do not include passwords or sensitive account information.

Can I suggest a feature or share feedback?

Yes. We welcome constructive feedback about gameplay, controls, accessibility, and the overall player experience. Send your suggestion by email and explain how it could improve the game. Suggestions help inform development, although we cannot promise that every request will be implemented.
